# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.0.0

First version.

- **No level cap in World 3** for every recruited character but Athena 2.0, as Rance: EXP and levels
  up to Rance's 999, ∞ on the cards and the status page.
- Nothing written into the save: in World 1 or 2, or without the mod, the game's caps hold again.
- Forbidden Talents and the game's other cap raises still raise a character's own cap in World 3.
- A box on its page in Config's Mods tab, kept per save, on at first.
